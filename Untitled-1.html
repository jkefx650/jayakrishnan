Smart Water Tank Monitoring System

Arduino-based project for automatic water level detection and alert system

Project Overview

This project is designed to monitor the water level in a tank using an ultrasonic sensor, display the information on an LCD screen, and trigger a buzzer when the tank reaches a warning or critical level.

Objectives

  • Measure water level automatically and accurately.
  • Display tank status on a 16x2 LCD display.
  • Alert the user when the water is near full or at risk.
  • Reduce manual checking and improve water management.

Components Used

  • Arduino UNO
  • HC-SR04 Ultrasonic Sensor
  • 16x2 I2C LCD Display
  • Buzzer
  • Jumper wires and breadboard
  • Power supply

Working Principle

The ultrasonic sensor sends out a sound pulse and measures the time for the echo to return. Using this time, the Arduino calculates the distance between the sensor and the water surface. The measured value is compared with preset thresholds to determine the water level.

System Logic

  • Trig Pin: 9
  • Echo Pin: 10
  • Buzzer Pin: 8
  • LCD: I2C 16x2 display
  • Warning Level: 90%
  • Alarm Level: 95%

Applications

  • Household water tanks
  • Industrial tank monitoring
  • Smart irrigation system
  • Water storage management

Conclusion

The Smart Water Tank Monitoring System is a practical embedded project that combines sensor-based measurement, Arduino control, and alert systems to provide an efficient and low-cost solution for monitoring water levels automatically.
